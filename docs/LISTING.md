# Marketplace listing draft

**Name:** Encore: Clips Your Audience Replayed

**Handle:** encore (or encore-clips if taken)

**Category:** Creative

**Tagline:** Turn one long video into captioned short clips for Shorts, TikTok, Reels and X, each with the reason it
was picked.

## Description

**What it does.** Finds the strongest moments in a long video and turns them into ready-to-post short clips.
When YouTube's "Most replayed" data exists, it uses that. Otherwise it reads the full transcript, and it tells
you which signal it used for every clip.

**What to send.**
- The video: a YouTube or other public link, or upload the file in the chat.
- Confirmation that you own the video or have the rights to it.
- Optional: the number of clips (default 5, up to 20), formats (9:16 vertical by default; 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9 also
  available), clip length (default 20 to 60 s), captions on or off (default on), and topics to focus on.

**What you get.** One MP4 per clip per format, with burned-in captions sized for phones. A report gives each
clip's source timestamps, why it was chosen, the key line quoted from it, and which signal was used.

**Run time.** About 10 to 30 minutes for a video up to 60 minutes. Sources up to 3 hours are accepted.

**How it communicates.** It confirms your request in the thread before starting and lists any defaults it used.
If something can't be done, such as a blocked link or an unsupported format, it says so plainly.

**What the price covers.** One video, the requested clips in every requested format, and change rounds on
named clips ("C2: start earlier", "replace C4"). Only the clips you name are re-made.

**What it won't do.** It won't clip videos you don't have rights to. It doesn't do music-only videos with no
speech, add B-roll, or post to your social accounts.
