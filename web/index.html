Picked by your viewers, not a guess

Many YouTube videos carry a public "Most replayed" graph. Encore reads it and cuts from the peaks. If a video has no replay data, it says so and works from the full transcript instead, looking for a hook, a complete thought with a payoff, and quotable lines.

Cuts that never land mid-word

Every clip starts and ends on a sentence boundary, inside the pause between words. An automated check rejects any cut that falls inside a word before the clip reaches you.

C2: start earlier

Change one clip, keep the rest

Clips have stable IDs. Reply "C2: start earlier", "replace C4" or "C1: end at 14:05" and Encore re-makes only the clips you named. Everything you already approved stays exactly as delivered.

How it works

Brief it like an editor. Get files back like one.

  1. 01

    Send the video

    A YouTube or other public link, or upload the file. Sources up to 3 hours. Say how many clips, which formats, how long, and which topics to focus on, or leave it to the defaults.

  2. 02

    Encore confirms the brief

    Before it starts, it replies with exactly what it will make and lists any defaults it filled in: 5 clips, 9:16, 20 to 60 seconds each, captions on.

  3. 03

    It finds and cuts the moments

    Replay peaks or the full transcript, sentence-safe cuts, framing that keeps the speaker in shot, captions from word timings. About 10 to 30 minutes for an hour-long video.

  4. 04

    Review, then revise by clip ID

    You get one MP4 per clip per format and a report with every clip's reason. Ask for changes by ID and only those clips are re-made.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What can I send?

A YouTube link, another public video link, or an uploaded file, up to 3 hours long. Encore needs speech to work with, so music-only videos aren't supported. Audio-only podcasts are fine.

What is "Most replayed" data, and what if my video doesn't have it?

YouTube publishes a replay graph on many videos showing which parts viewers rewatched most. Encore uses it when it's there. When it isn't, or the graph is flat, Encore chooses from the full timestamped transcript. Every clip's report says which signal it used.

What if my link can't be downloaded?

YouTube sometimes blocks downloads from servers. If that happens Encore tells you plainly, and you can upload the file instead.

How long does a job take?

About 10 to 30 minutes for a video up to an hour. Longer sources take longer.

Can I change a clip I don't like?

Yes. Reply with the clip ID and what to change: "C2: start earlier", "C4: replace with a different moment", "C1: end at 14:05", "bigger captions on C3". Only the clips you name are re-made.

What gets sent to AI models?

Speech-to-text receives the audio, and the model that picks moments receives the transcript text. Cutting, framing and captioning run in Encore's own video pipeline.

Do I need the rights to the video?

Yes. Encore only clips videos you own or have permission to use, and asks you to confirm that with each job.

How much does it cost?

Today Encore is hired per video on NEAR Agent Market. Monthly plans priced by hours of video are planned; the prices on this page are placeholders until they launch.

Does Encore post the clips for me?

No. You get the files and the report, and you decide what goes out.
